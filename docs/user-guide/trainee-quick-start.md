# Trainee quick start

DRAFT, 2026-11-26. All wording in the exercises is PLACEHOLDER until your unit approves it.

## 1. Log in

[Screenshot: login dialog, trainee ID list]

- Tap **Log in** in the top bar. Choose your **trainee ID**. Trainees need no PIN.
- Without a login you are a **guest**: you can practise, but your results are in nobody's history.
- When you finish, tap **Log out** in the top bar.

## 2. Choose your exercise

[Screenshot: Dashboard with scenario card and "Choose plan"]

- On the **Dashboard**, tap **Choose plan**. You see your own plans and plans for anyone.
- The card shows what will run: **callsign, aircraft type, runway, taxiway**, and the instructor's briefing. Read it before you start.
- Tap **START EXERCISE**. The Start dialog shows the same values again, and whether it is **Practice** (you see how you did at once) or **Assessed** (you see it at the end).

## 3. Talk on the radio: the foot pedal

[Screenshot: radio bar with TX and RX]

- **Press and hold** the pedal, **then** speak, and **release** after your last word.
- **TX** (amber) means that you are transmitting. **RX** (cyan) means that the pilot is speaking.
- **WAIT** means that the pilot is still speaking: wait, then press again.
- If the pedal does not work, use **PTT** on the screen. The instructor can help.

## 4. Speaking rules

- Start with the **callsign**, then the instruction: *"Eagle four two one, taxi via alpha three, runway zero nine."*
- Say numbers digit by digit: "four two one", "zero nine".
- One instruction per transmission. Use standard phraseology, at an even pace and normal loudness.
- Wait for the **readback**. If it is wrong, correct it. If the pilot says "say again", repeat clearly.
- If nobody answers, check the callsign: the pilot answers only to its own callsign.

## 5. Read the result

[Screenshot: Result screen]

- **Steps**: done, missed, and how many attempts each took.
- **Response time**: how quickly you answered when it was your turn.
- **PTT**: for example speaking before pressing, or releasing too early.
- **Recognition**: where the machine may have misheard you. This is shown **apart** from your errors and is never counted against you.
- **Run again** starts the same plan. With random values, the new values are shown before you start.
